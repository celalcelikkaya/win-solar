What happened

    New in this version

    • NewFollows the real light. Uses the PC's light sensor, or the weather when there is none. Cloudy evenings go dark earlier, grey mornings stay dark longer.
    • NewWallpaper that moves with the sun. Night, dawn, sunrise, morning, noon, afternoon, golden hour and dusk.
    • NewDay and Night profiles. Accent color, mouse pointer, sounds and wallpaper switch together.
    • NewBattery mode. Goes dark below 20% or whenever unplugged.
    • NewFollows you when you travel. A new PC time zone updates the location.
    • Opens like a Windows flyout. Slides in from the taskbar, on whichever edge it is.
    • Warning as a real notification. 5 minutes before a switch, with Snooze, Skip and Switch now, even when the window is closed.
    • Snooze and Skip survive a restart. Try Restart while a switch is postponed.
    • Live tray icon. Sun by day, moon by night. Hover for the countdown, right-click for the menu.
    • Shortcut. Win + Shift + D switches the theme (Shift + D here).
    • Never interrupts full screen. Videos, games and slideshows finish first, then the theme switches.
    • Taskbar can keep one theme while apps switch.
    • Fixed times as an alternative to sunrise and sunset.
    • Quiet restarts and in-app updates. Nothing is reapplied when the theme is already right.
